# Core EDA

Core EDA produces reproducible Parquet artifacts rather than notebook-only results. It includes statewide and municipal establishment series, municipality shares/ranks, SCIAN sector shares and location quotients, size-band composition, sector HHI/Shannon/effective diversity, contact-channel coverage and weighted data-quality shares.

Growth around known full rebenchmark editions (2015-01, 2019-11, 2024-11) is retained but excluded from the default continuity growth diagnostic. Robust growth outliers use median/MAD and are descriptive flags, not automatic deletions.
